# Governance

OpenPatch is maintained by its repository maintainers. Maintainers set the roadmap, review contributions, manage releases, and may delegate those responsibilities.

Technical decisions are made in public issues and pull requests whenever practical. A maintainer makes the final decision when consensus is not reached, with priority given to user safety, privacy, maintainability, and the project scope.

New maintainers may be invited based on sustained, constructive contributions and demonstrated familiarity with the project. Maintainers may step down at any time.
